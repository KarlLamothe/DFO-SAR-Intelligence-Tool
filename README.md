# DFO Freshwater SAR Intelligence Tool

A reproducible R-based platform for compiling, organizing,
and exploring information on Canadian freshwater fish species
listed under the Species at Risk Act (SARA).

## Objectives

- Maintain a current database of freshwater fishes at risk
- Track recovery planning documents
- Summarize threats and recovery actions
- Explore species distributions
- Support DFO science, recovery planning, and offset reviews

## Planned Components

- Data harvesting scripts
- SQLite database
- Shiny dashboard
- Automated species reports

## Current Outputs

### freshwater_species_master.csv
Core freshwater fish inventory.

### species_profiles.csv
Species profile information including:
- Description
- Population
- Habitat
- Biology
- Threats
- Other protection

### species_full.csv
Combined species inventory and profile information.
